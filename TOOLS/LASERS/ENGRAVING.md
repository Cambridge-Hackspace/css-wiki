# Laser Engraving & Cutting Workshop

## Overview

Laser cutting and engraving has become a popular tool for makers creating a
variety of projects for arts, crafts, and technology. This workshop introduces
the basics of laser engraving/etching and cutting through a hands-on project:
laser-cut and engraved coasters.

Throughout the event you will use [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/en/download/),
a free design tool, to personalize your coaster by adding text, an image, or a
photo. You'll learn how to operate the laser cutter at Cambridge Hackspace using
both engraving/etch and cut modes. By the end of the workshop you will leave with
your own laser-cut coasters.

Although no design or graphic-artist experience is necessary to participate, we
recommend that students become familiar with Inkscape. The emphasis of this
workshop is not on designing or graphic arts; rather, it focuses on processing
the final design for laser engraving and cutting.

## What we provide

1. Wood material for the coasters.
2. The laser cutter system at Cambridge Hackspace.

## What you must bring

1. A working copy of [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/en/download/) (for PC or
   Mac), downloaded and installed.
2. A willingness to experiment with your design.

## Age requirement

Participants must be 18 or over. Children under the age of 18 must be accompanied
by a parent or legal guardian.
