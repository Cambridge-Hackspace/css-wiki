# Moving / Disassembling the CO₂ Laser

## Overview

How to split the CO₂ laser cutter into its top and base so it can fit through a
doorway narrower than the machine. Condensed from Boss Laser work instruction
**WI-855-700, Rev. 001 (6/1/19)**. The machines are **heavy** — plan a team and a
moving dolly before you start.

## Procedure

1. Open the large white access doors around the bottom of the machine (left, right, and front).
2. With those doors open you can reach the Allen screws that fasten the top of the machine to the base. They are **6 mm** Allen screws — **8 total**, two each at the front, left, right, and back.
3. Loosen those screws and pull them out of their holes.
4. **Before** lifting the top off the base, remove everything inside the machine — the honeycomb table and all the knife blades. This matters because the machine gets laid on its back to clear the doorway, and anything loose inside can fall onto the head, mirrors, belts, or motors.
5. Once the inside is clear, position a moving dolly behind the machine, then lift the machine off and onto its back onto the dolly. These machines are very heavy, so have a good team to do this safely.
