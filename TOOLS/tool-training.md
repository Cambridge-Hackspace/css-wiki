# Tool Training

To use any of our power tools, you'll need to be trained by our training team.

Tools include:

 -  Laser cutter
 -  CNCs
 -  3D printers
 -  Powered woodworking tools
 -  Any other tool that uses mains power.

Training is not required for:

 -  Soldering irons
 -  Power supplies
 -  Oscilloscopes

Training can be arrange by emailing [training@cambridgehackspace.com](mailto:training@cambridgehackspace.com)

They'll arrange a time to come in, go through the training material, and get you setup.
