## Safety Guidelines
- Turn on the fume extractor!
- Never touch the hot tip (can reach 300-400°C)
- Use appropriate holder when iron is not in hand
- Wash hands after soldering (especially with leaded solder)
- Wear safety glasses
- Keep flammable materials away from work area
- Let iron cool completely before storing

## Getting Started
1. Turn on iron and set appropriate temperature (typically 300-350°C)
2. Wait for iron to heat up
3. Tin the tip with fresh solder
4. Position components
5. Heat the joint (not the solder)
6. Apply solder to the heated joint
7. Remove solder, then remove iron
8. Allow joint to cool without movement

## Good Solder Joints
- Shiny, smooth appearance
- Concave fillet shape
- Complete wetting to both surfaces
- No cold joints (dull, grainy appearance)
- No solder bridges between pins

## Maintenance
- Clean tip regularly while soldering
- Tin tip before turning off
- Replace tips when worn
- Report any issues to workshop staff

## Troubleshooting
- **Solder won't stick**: Clean surfaces, use flux, increase temperature
- **Cold joints**: Increase temperature or heating time
- **Solder bridges**: Use less solder, use solder wick to remove excess
