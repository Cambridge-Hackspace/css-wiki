# Cricut Maker 3

## Overview

The **Cricut Maker 3** uses swappable blades that can cut, score, and engrave a variety of materials. Its pen/marker attachment can also be used to create calligraphy or drawings.

## What can I make with this?

Use the pen attachment to do motorized calligraphy.

Below is a non-exhaustive list of Cricut blades and their compatible materials.

| Blade Type | Compatible Materials | Do we own this? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium fine point blade | vinyl, stencil film, iron-on vinyl, paper, cardstock, sticker paper, normal paper, vellum, foil, acetate | Yes |
| Rotary blade | cotton, silk, lycra, denim, felt, chiffon, cashmere, crepe paper | Yes |
| Knife blade | genuine leather, pleather, chipboard, balsa, basswood | Not yet |

## Using the Cutting Mats

Each cutting mat color corresponds to a different material type. The mats have an adhesive property that fixes the material in one position while cutting. When a mat looks dirty, wash it with Dawn dish soap and a sponge; it will become sticky again once it has dried.

| Mat Color | Adhesive Strength | Intended Materials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Blue LightGrip Mat** | Least adhesion | Tissue paper, light cardstock, patterned paper, construction paper, and vellum |
| **Green StandardGrip Mat** | Medium adhesion | Vinyl, sticker paper, thicker cardstocks, etc. |
| **Pink FabricGrip Mat** | Strong adhesion | Leather, faux leather, felt, fabric, etc. |
