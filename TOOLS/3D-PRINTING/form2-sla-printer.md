# Form 2 SLA 3D Printer

## Overview
The Form 2 is a stereolithography (SLA) 3D printer that uses a laser to cure liquid resin into solid parts with high precision and smooth surface finish.

## Specifications
- **Brand**: Formlabs
- **Model**: Form 2
- **Build Volume**: 145 × 145 × 175 mm
- **Layer Thickness**: 25, 50, or 100 microns
- **Laser Spot Size**: 140 microns

## Safety Guidelines
- **Wear gloves** when handling uncured resin (skin irritant)
- **Wear safety glasses** when working with resin
- Work in well-ventilated area
- Avoid skin contact with resin
- Wash hands thoroughly after use
- Dispose of resin waste properly

## Workflow
1. Upload model to PreForm software
2. Orient and add supports
3. Send print to Form 2
4. Allow print to complete
5. Remove build platform
6. Wash part in IPA (isopropyl alcohol)
7. Remove supports
8. Post-cure if necessary
