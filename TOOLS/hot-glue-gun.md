# Hot Glue Gun

## Overview
Electrically heated tool that melts solid glue sticks for quick bonding of various materials.

## Specifications
- A small Surebonder which will help you glue lots of things!

## Best Uses
- Crafts and decorations
- Quick prototyping
- Temporary fixtures
- Fabric and felt projects
- Lightweight materials
- Floral arrangements
- Model building

## Not Recommended For
- High-stress structural bonds
- Heat-sensitive materials
- Outdoor applications (UV degrades glue)
- Items that will experience high temperatures
- Smooth plastic surfaces (poor adhesion)

## Safety Guidelines
- **Hot glue can cause serious burns** (reaches 180-200°C)
- Never touch nozzle or melted glue
- Always use stand when not holding gun
- Unplug when finished
- Allow to cool completely before storing
- Work on heat-resistant surface
- Keep away from water when plugged in
- Don't leave unattended while heated

## Using a Hot Glue Gun
1. Insert glue stick into rear of gun
2. Plug in and allow to heat up
3. Test on scrap material to ensure glue flows
4. Apply glue to surface
5. Quickly press surfaces together
6. Hold for 10-30 seconds until set
7. Allow full cure (several minutes)
8. Unplug and allow to cool

## Tips and Tricks
- Work quickly - glue sets fast
- Apply glue to heavier/less heat-sensitive item first
- Use in well-ventilated area
- Keep paper towels handy for drips
- Glue strings can be removed after cooling
- Pre-roughen smooth surfaces for better adhesion
- For detailed work, use low-temp gun

## Maintenance
- Clean nozzle while warm (not hot) with aluminum foil
- Never pull glue stick out backward
- Store upright or in provided tray
- Check cord for damage
- Report any issues to workshop staff

## Troubleshooting
- **Glue won't flow**: Allow more heat-up time, check for old hardened glue
- **Glue dripping**: Normal for most guns, use stand to catch drips
- **Poor adhesion**: Clean surfaces, roughen smooth materials, work faster
- **Glue stringing**: Use lower temperature if available

## Cleaning Up
- Allow glue to fully cool and harden
- Peel off excess glue (usually comes off cleanly)
- For stubborn residue: rubbing alcohol or heat to soften
- Clean work surface after use
