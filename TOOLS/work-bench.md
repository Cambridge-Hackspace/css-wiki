## Safety
- Keep work area organized to prevent accidents
- Use appropriate tools for the task
- Wear safety equipment as needed
- Ensure adequate lighting
- Don't block emergency exits or paths
- Report any hazards or spills immediately

## Etiquette
- Don't monopolize bench space
- Communicate with others sharing the space
- Keep noise to reasonable levels
- Respect others' projects and materials
- Clean up fully before leaving

## What NOT to Do
- Leave messes or spills
- Store projects indefinitely
- Use as general storage
- Block access for others
- Leave tools or equipment unattended while running
