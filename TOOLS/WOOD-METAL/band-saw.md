# Band Saw

## Overview
Safety training reference for the band saw in the Cambridge Hackspace woodshop. Review this before operating the tool, and complete the required in-person training. See the [Woodworking Safety Policy](TOOLS/WOOD-METAL/SAFETY.md) for general shop rules and [Wood Dust Safety](TOOLS/WOOD-METAL/DUST.md).

## Safety Training

| Job/Task Step | Hazard | Hazard Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inspect Equipment/PPE Check | N/A | Don all required PPE for use of the band saw: approved safety glasses, long pants, closed toe shoes.<br>Ear plugs are recommended.<br>Tuck in shirt, no loose fitting clothing or jewelry.<br>Long hair must be pulled back or tucked into a hat. |
| Place the material on the band saw platform | Abrasions/Lacerations, Pinch Points, Muscle Strain | Wear gloves to prevent cuts if handling material that is sharp or has ragged edges. |
| Adjust blade guard to the desired height | Abrasions/Lacerations, Pinch Points, Moving Parts | The guard should expose only the minimum amount of saw blade needed to cut your workpiece. Be aware of pinch point hazards while the adjustment movement is being made. |
| **STOP — PPE CHECK** | **PPE CHECK** | PRIOR TO TURNING ON THE SAW AND MAKING THE CUT, ENSURE ALL REQUIRED PPE IS WORN.<br>Safety glasses prevent eye injury from flying debris.<br>Long pants and closed toe shoes prevent injury from falling objects being cut.<br>Ear plugs are recommended due to high noise hazards. |
| Turn the saw on / make the cut | Flying Objects, Abrasion/Laceration, High Noise, Falling Objects, Slipping Hazards | Be aware of saw dust, metal filings and other debris that is falling on the ground.<br>Keep fingers clear of the moving blade.<br>Be mindful of the potential of slip/trip/fall hazards. |
| Turn off the saw | Moving Parts | Do not attempt to touch the blade of the saw or remove your cut material until the blade has stopped moving completely.<br>Continue to wear all required PPE: safety glasses, closed toe shoes, and long pants. |
| Remove cut material from the saw's platform | Abrasions/Lacerations, Pinch Points, Muscle Strain | Wear gloves to prevent cuts if handling material that is sharp or has ragged edges.<br>Use proper lifting techniques and ask for help should the material be too heavy or awkward to lift. |
| Clean work area | Abrasions/Lacerations, Flying Debris | Wear gloves while picking up large or sharp pieces of cut material for disposal.<br>Wear safety glasses to prevent eye injury while sweeping the area on and under the saw. |
| Move the blade shield down to conceal the blade | Moving Parts | Be aware of hand and body placement. |
