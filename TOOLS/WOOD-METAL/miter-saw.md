# Miter Saw

## Overview
Safety training reference for the miter saw in the Cambridge Hackspace woodshop. Review this before operating the tool, and complete the required in-person training. See the [Woodworking Safety Policy](TOOLS/WOOD-METAL/SAFETY.md) for general shop rules and [Wood Dust Safety](TOOLS/WOOD-METAL/DUST.md).

## Safety Training

| Job/Task Step | Hazard | Hazard Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inspect Equipment/PPE Check/Buddy System | N/A | Don all required PPE for use of the miter saw: approved safety glasses with side shields, long pants, and closed toe shoes.<br>Consider ear plugs for noise protection.<br>Additional foot protection is required when working with heavy parts and pieces.<br>Tuck in shirt, no loose fitting clothing or jewelry.<br>Long hair must be pulled back or tucked into a hat.<br>Ensure guard is working properly. |
| Prepare wood for cutting | Abrasions/Lacerations, Pinch Points, Muscle Strains, Falling Objects | Be aware of hand and body placement when handling material to prevent hand injury and pinch points.<br>If the wood has sharp edges or splinters, wear gloves.<br>Use proper lifting techniques when lifting material.<br>Ask for help if material is too heavy or awkward.<br>Wear closed toe shoes and long pants to prevent injury should the material be dropped or fall. |
| Make the cut | Abrasions/Lacerations, Noise Hazards, Flying/Falling Objects, Muscle Strains, Slip/Trip/Fall | Be aware of hand and body placement to prevent injury when pushing material to make the cut.<br>Ensure wood is flush against the back plate when making the cut.<br>Be aware of the location of the cord to prevent electrical hazards.<br>Saw will produce high noise. Consider wearing ear plugs while making the cut.<br>To prevent eye injury, wear safety glasses during the cut due to flying sawdust.<br>Wear a dust mask to prevent breathing in dust and debris.<br>Wear closed toe shoes and long pants to prevent injury from falling objects.<br>Ensure the area where the cut is being made is clear of obstructions to prevent slip/trip/fall hazards. |
| Turn off the saw | Abrasions/Lacerations | The blade will not stop immediately. |
| Remove cut wood | Abrasions/Lacerations, Pinch Points, Muscle Strains, Falling Objects | Be aware of hand and body placement when handling material to prevent hand injury and pinch points.<br>Wear gloves if the wood has sharp edges or splinters.<br>Use proper lifting techniques when removing material.<br>Ask for help if material is too heavy or awkward.<br>Wear closed toe shoes and long pants to prevent injury should the material be dropped or fall. |
| Clean the area | Abrasions/Lacerations, Flying Objects, Muscle Strains | Wear safety glasses while sweeping the area of sawdust to prevent eye injury.<br>Use proper lifting technique when lifting trash and debris from the cutting site. |
