# Scroll Saw

## Overview
Safety training reference for the scroll saw in the Cambridge Hackspace woodshop. Review this before operating the tool, and complete the required in-person training. See the [Woodworking Safety Policy](TOOLS/WOOD-METAL/SAFETY.md) for general shop rules and [Wood Dust Safety](TOOLS/WOOD-METAL/DUST.md).

## Safety Training

| Job/Task Step | Hazard | Hazard Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inspect Equipment/PPE Check/Buddy System | N/A | Ensure guard is working properly.<br>Wear gloves if the wood has sharp edges or splinters. |
| Prepare wood for cutting | Abrasions/Lacerations, Pinch Points, Muscle Strains, Falling Objects | Lower the guard so it sits snugly on top of the wood.<br>If the material has sharp edges or splinters, wear gloves. |
| Make the cut | Abrasions/Lacerations, Noise Hazards, Flying/Falling Objects, Muscle Strains, Slip/Trip/Fall | Wear a dust mask to prevent breathing in dust and debris.<br>Ensure the area where the cut is being made is clear of obstructions to prevent slip/trip/fall hazards. |
| Turn off the saw | Abrasions/Lacerations | The blade will not stop immediately. |
| Remove cut wood | Abrasions/Lacerations, Pinch Points, Muscle Strains, Falling Objects | Be aware of hand and body placement when handling material to prevent hand injury and pinch points.<br>Use proper lifting techniques when removing material. |
| Clean the area | Abrasions/Lacerations, Flying Objects, Muscle Strains | Wear safety glasses while sweeping the area of sawdust to prevent eye injury.<br>Use proper lifting technique when lifting trash and debris from the cutting site. |
