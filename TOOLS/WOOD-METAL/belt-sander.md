# Belt Sander

## Overview
Safety training reference for the belt sander in the Cambridge Hackspace woodshop. This tool is only to be used on wood. Review this before operating the tool, and complete the required in-person training. See the [Woodworking Safety Policy](TOOLS/WOOD-METAL/SAFETY.md) for general shop rules and [Wood Dust Safety](TOOLS/WOOD-METAL/DUST.md).

## Safety Training

| Job/Task Step | Hazard | Hazard Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inspect Equipment/PPE Check/Buddy System/Dust Abatement | N/A | Consider a face mask to reduce inhalation of airborne sawdust.<br>Ensure area is clear in front of the grinder to prevent slip/trip/fall hazards. |
| Start the machine and slowly contact material to the sanding belt. This tool is only to be used on wood. | Abrasions/Laceration, Pinch Points, Flying Objects, Eye Injury, Noise Hazard | Hold tightly onto the material being sanded.<br>Use the base of the machine in front of the belt to support the material being sanded to prevent kick back.<br>Be aware of flying objects caused from sanding activities. |
| Turn off the grinder and clean up the area | Abrasions/Laceration, Pinch Points, Flying Debris, Muscle Strains | Belt will not immediately stop. Do not touch the belt to force it to stop.<br>Wait for the belt to completely stop turning prior to moving on. |
