# Arduino Workshops

## Overview

Build-and-take-home workshops around the Arduino. Components, soldering tools
and instruction are provided, and you keep what you build. No prior soldering
experience is assumed — [Build your own Arduino](WORKSHOPS/ARDUINO/BUILD.md) is
the one to start with.

Every project ships with its source: you control the code, so you control what
the finished thing does.

## Workshops

- [Build your own Arduino](WORKSHOPS/ARDUINO/BUILD.md) — learn to solder and take home a working Arduino.
- [Build your own Arduino Clock](WORKSHOPS/ARDUINO/CLOCK.md) — a 4-character 7-segment display with a battery-backed calendar chip, so it keeps time while unplugged. About three hours.
- [Build your own Arduino Sound Meter](WORKSHOPS/ARDUINO/SOUND.md) — samples the room, runs a Fourier transform, and shows the levels on an 8x8 bi-colour LED bar graph.
- [Build your own Arduino Temperature Sensor](WORKSHOPS/ARDUINO/TEMP.md) — similar build to the clock; a button switches units and resets the minimum/maximum readings.
