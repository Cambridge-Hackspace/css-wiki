# 3D Printing Workshops

## Overview

Workshops covering the design side of 3D printing — going from an idea to a
model you can put on a printer. For the printers themselves, their filaments and
the printing workflow, see [3D Printing](TOOLS/3D-PRINTING/INDEX.md) under Tools.

## Workshops

- [3d Drawing](WORKSHOPS/3D/DRAWING.md) — back to basics drawing, from stick men to 3D models, ending with something ready to print.
- [Design and Print a 3D object](WORKSHOPS/3D/PRINTING.md) — the basic design skills for constructing and manipulating your own objects, then printing them.
