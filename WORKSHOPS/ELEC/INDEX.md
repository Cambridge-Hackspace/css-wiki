# Electronics Workshops

## Overview

Soldering and wearable-electronics workshops. For the bench equipment, the
components on hand and the tools themselves, see
[Electronics](TOOLS/ELECTRONICS/INDEX.md) under Tools.

Arduino build workshops have their own section:
[Arduino](WORKSHOPS/ARDUINO/INDEX.md).

## Workshops

- [Sew your own LED gloves](WORKSHOPS/ELEC/GLOVES.md) — the basics of sewable electronics, from planning a design to walking out in LED-lit clothes.
- [SMD soldering workshop](WORKSHOPS/ELEC/SMD.md) — soldering and desoldering surface-mount parts with a standard iron.
