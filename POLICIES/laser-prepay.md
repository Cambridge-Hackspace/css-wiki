# How to Handle Prepaying $100 on ToolPass for the Laser Cutter

## Overview

Procedure for charging a member the $100 laser cutter prepayment on ToolPass and setting their discounted rate on both laser cutters.

## Charge $100

Click the **…** to the right of the balance, then click **Charge Member on Stripe**.

![Charge Member on Stripe dialog with the amount set to 100.00 and the Charge button highlighted](assets/policies/laser-prepay/img01.png)

## Set the Discounted Rate on Both Laser Cutters

Click **Update Rate**, then click **Set to Discounted** for both the small and large laser cutter.

![Update Rate dialog with "Set to Discounted" selected](assets/policies/laser-prepay/img02.png)
