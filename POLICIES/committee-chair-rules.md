# Special Interest Committee Chair Function and Rules

## Overview

Defines the role, election, and spending rules for Special Interest Committee Chairs, giving members more ownership of the space and creating accountability for areas to stay better kept, with updated trainer lists and budget autonomy to advocate for needed tools to be purchased or replaced.

Adapted from the [Dallas Makerspace rules for committees](https://dallasmakerspace.org/wiki/Rules_and_Policies#Committees) to work for our space/membership size.

## Special Interest Committees

- Fiber Arts
- 3D Printing
- Laser Cutting
- Wood Working
- Metal Working
- Electronics

## What a Committee Chair Does

### Committee Leadership

1. Committees shall have a Chair who will serve until a Chair-Elect is nominated by the members during a Chair Election, dismissal of the Chair by the Board of Directors, or the Board of Directors appoints a replacement Chair.
2. Duties of the Committee Chair:
   1. Maintaining the safety of the Committee area, including ensuring the safe operation of all tooling.
   2. Maintaining good order of the committee area and resources, ensuring adequate supplies of consumable items are maintained.
   3. Any items the Chair wishes to get rid of must follow the Cambridge Hackspace [Procedure to Remove Assets](POLICIES/removing-assets.md).
   4. Enforcing the rules established by the committee, and of the Cambridge Hackspace within their area, such as required tool training and safety.
   5. Maintaining a list of active trainers in the [List of Trainers](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1YqFDUOJla5xatS7Dm9vfxoL2fEwpXwU3NpPekYrbuJU/edit?gid=0#gid=0) on the drive.
3. The Committee Chair has the option to appoint a Vice Chair.
   - Duties of the Vice Chair:
     1. Assist the Chair, as determined by the Chair or by the Committee at large.
     2. Provide oversight and supervision of the Committee area when the Chair is not present.
     3. Serve as Interim Chair when:
        1. The Chair is not available for an extended period.
        2. The Chair has resigned.

### Chair Elections

1. Committee elections shall occur as part of the annual member meeting, or a special election if a chair is needed.
2. Members shall meet and vote on a Chair to nominate for confirmation by the Board of Directors.
   - Nominees do not need to be present to be nominated and elected.
3. The winning candidate shall become Chair-Elect and assume the duties of the Chair.
4. The Board of Directors shall consider the committee's nomination of the Chair-Elect at the soonest convenient Board Meeting, and may opt to do one of the following:
   1. Confirm the Chair-Elect, upon which the Chair-Elect and, if applicable, Vice Chair-Elect become Chair and Vice Chair, respectively.
   2. Reject the nomination of the Chair-Elect and appoint a different Chair, who may name their own Vice-Chair.
   3. Reject the nomination of the Chair-Elect and direct the members to hold another election.

### Committee Chair Responsibilities and Rules

1. The Committee budget is set by the board and can be adjusted with a board vote.
2. The Committee's fund balance is managed by the Treasurer.
3. The Committee Chair has the ability to purchase items for their committee within guidelines preapproved by the Board of Directors:
   1. The Committee Chair may make purchases of supplies and/or tooling without a Committee vote, for purchases in amounts less than $100, or 1/3 of the Committee's total fund balance, whichever is smaller.
   2. Purchases for items exceeding $100 require a vote by members.
   3. Reimbursement for purchases requires a receipt sent to the Treasurer.
4. Committees are responsible for establishing the rules of the Committee area.
5. Committees may establish other positions as needed to ensure the effective operation of the committee, or to accomplish Committee goals. Ad Hoc positions with spending authority, or administrative access to Cambridge Hackspace resources, require the approval of the Board.
