# How to Use the Dumpster

## Overview

Rules for using the Cambridge Hackspace dumpster and handling trash in the space. Keeping our trash disposal service happy is important so that we can keep having a dumpster.

## Dumpster Rules

- All trash must be secured in bags or boxes
- All trash must be **inside** the dumpster. If the dumpster is full, see below and take the trash back inside until there is room.
- No furniture or construction material
- No hazardous waste (paint, batteries, chemicals, etc.)
  - *You* must take these to your local DPW
  - Please do not keep or use any dangerous chemicals in the Hackspace
- The lid must be **locked tightly** when you are finished
  - People walking by will try to put stuff in the dumpster and it makes a mess, costs us money, and makes our trash service unhappy about the loose trash

**If the dumpster is nearly full,** please email [trash@cambridgehackspace.com](mailto:trash@cambridgehackspace.com) so we can schedule a pickup. The regularly scheduled pickup is the 2nd Tuesday of the month.

**The dumpster key** is kept on the wall near the door. It has a tag with the dumpster rules engraved on it. Please return it when you are finished taking out the trash.

## General Trash Rules in the Hackspace

If the trash can is nearly full, it is time to take out the trash. Compacting trash is dangerous and makes it too heavy to take out easily, so stay safe and take it out now. If you put something heavy in the trash, check how much the bin weighs and take it out if it would be hard for someone less strong than you to lift it into the bin.
