# Flat Storage Area

## Overview

Rules for using the flat storage area, including how long items may be left before they are moved to the free pile.

## Rules

Things must be **clearly** labeled with your name and the date when it was last used. Things which have not been used in 3 months will be moved to the free pile.

For example, a thing labeled:

Jane Doe 2025-05-17

Will be moved to the free pile on 2025-08-17.
