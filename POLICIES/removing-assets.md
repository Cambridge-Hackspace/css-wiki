# Procedure to Remove Assets

## Overview

A streamlined guide for the board of directors to determine how to remove excess assets from the hackspace, with the purpose of keeping assets out of the landfill, generating revenue, and/or freeing up space.

## Definitions

**Asset** — "anything you own that has value and could be converted into cash". In the case of Cambridge Hackspace this is mostly tools, equipment, and in some cases, specialty raw materials like high value lumber.

## Procedure

### How to Mark an Asset for Removal

- Move the asset to the free pile, or label it with a sticker/tape as free.

### How to Determine the Value of the Asset

**Who owns the asset?**

1. A member
2. On loan to the Cambridge Hackspace
3. The Cambridge Hackspace

**If A or B:** Can we contact the owner to remove the item(s)?

**If C:** Does the asset have an assigned value? Refer to [Hackspace Assets](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/d/1qINErB1xfzBOlU2QiDuq61EUpfOs4U8XA3rGGcVcdVQ/edit).

- Yes — use the assigned value.
- If no, determine the value:
  - If the projected value is > $100, confer with the board to agree upon a value.
  - If the projected value is < $100, assign a value at will.
  - If the asset does not have an apparent value but is still functional / usable to the right person (i.e. not junk to be thrown out), try to give it away for free.

### How to Sell/Give Away the Asset

Using the assigned value from the section above, reach out to the following groups in order of priority.

Whoever agrees has 1 week to pick up. Tuesday night open build night is the preferred pickup time for non-members.

To reach out to Milk Row Studios, use MilkRow@googlegroups.com.

### How to Deposit the Money to Cambridge Hackspace

- Contact the Cambridge Hackspace Treasurer.
