# Receiving Donations

## Overview

Channels through which Cambridge Hackspace can collect donations and pursue grant funding.

## Amazon

Amazon allows us to collect donations, either one-off or recurring.

<https://payments.amazon.com/business/asp>

## PayPal

PayPal seems like another alternative.

<https://cms.paypal.com/us/cgi-bin/marketingweb?cmd=_render-content&content_ID=merchant/donations>

## Google Ad Grants

When we are 501c3, we can apply for Google Ad Grants, giving us free advertising up to $10,000 a month — fairly impressive!

<http://www.google.com/grants/index.html>
